## Research Rules

- Check this file before browsing.
- Browse when the relevant evidence is absent, stale, contradicted, local/current, or explicitly requested.
- Prefer academic papers, consensus statements, sports nutrition bodies, universities, and official institutions.
- For budget food planning, current local prices may require browsing or user-provided prices.
- Do not store long quotes. Store short summaries and practical takeaways.
- Do not mix the user's dislikes or adherence notes into evidence memory.
